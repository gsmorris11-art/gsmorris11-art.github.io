Before you make an offer in Fredericksburg

  1. Get fully pre-approved. In a competitive market, reviewed income and assets carry more weight with a listing agent than a pre-qualification letter.
  2. Pull your Certificate of Eligibility. Your lender can usually get it in minutes if you are using VA.
  3. Count HOA dues and commuting costs. Both belong in the monthly budget you set for yourself.
  4. Compare lenders on the same day. Rates move daily, so same-day Loan Estimates are the only fair comparison.

Fredericksburg mortgage questions

Can I buy a home in Fredericksburg with no down payment?

Eligible veterans and service members can with a VA loan. USDA loans also need no down payment, but the city and the built-up parts of Stafford and Spotsylvania are generally outside USDA's eligible area. FHA starts at 3.5% down and some conventional loans at 3%.

What are the 2026 loan limits in Fredericksburg, VA?

For a single-family home, the conforming limit and the FHA limit are both $1,249,125 in Fredericksburg, Spotsylvania County and Stafford County, because the area is part of the Washington metro. Limits are reset each year.

Can I use a VA loan on a condo or townhome in Fredericksburg?

Yes. Townhomes are treated like any other home. Condos must be in a development on VA's approved list, which your loan officer can check before you make an offer.

How fast can I close if I am on PCS orders?

Most purchase loans close in about 30 days from contract. Being fully pre-approved before you start looking is the best way to hold that timeline.
