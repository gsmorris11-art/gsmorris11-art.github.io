What makes a Madison County mortgage different

Lenders who mostly see subdivision homes can stall on these. Raising them at the start keeps closing on schedule.

Wells and septic

Public water and sewer are rare outside the Town of Madison. Plan a water test and a septic inspection early in the contract period.

Acreage and comparable sales

Appraisers need recent sales of similar rural properties. On large or unusual parcels that search takes longer, so order the appraisal promptly.

Private roads and long driveways

Homes reached by a shared or private road may need a recorded road maintenance agreement before closing.

Land-use taxation

Parcels taxed under the county's land-use program can owe rollback taxes when the use changes. Ask about it before you buy land to build on.

Before you make an offer in Madison County

  1. Check the address for USDA. It takes a minute and can remove the down payment entirely.
  2. Get fully pre-approved. Sellers of rural property want to know the financing will hold through a well, septic and appraisal review.
  3. Ask what conveys. Barns, sheds, fencing and extra parcels affect the appraisal and sometimes the loan type.
  4. Budget for inspections. Water, septic and, for older farmhouses, a full home inspection.

Madison County mortgage questions

Is Madison County, VA eligible for USDA home loans?

Much of Madison County falls inside USDA's eligible rural area, but eligibility is set by the specific address and by household income. Scott's team can check a property against the USDA map before you write an offer.

What are the 2026 loan limits in Madison County, VA?

For a single-family home the 2026 FHA limit is $541,287 and the conforming loan limit is $1,209,750. Limits are reset each calendar year by HUD and FHFA.

Can I get a mortgage on a house with acreage in Madison County?

Yes. Conventional, FHA, VA and USDA loans all finance homes on acreage as long as the property is residential in nature and the appraiser can find comparable sales. Working farms that produce income are handled differently.

Can I finance land and build a home in Madison County?

Yes. A one-time-close construction loan covers the land, the build and the permanent mortgage with a single closing. The builder, plans and budget are approved before closing.
