What makes a Fauquier County mortgage different

The same loan can go smoothly in a Bealeton subdivision and need extra work on a farm outside Marshall.

Horse and hobby-farm properties

Residential loans can finance a home with barns and pasture when the property is mainly a residence. Tell your lender about outbuildings and any farm income up front.

HOA neighborhoods

Communities around New Baltimore, Vint Hill and Bealeton carry association dues, which lenders count in your monthly payment.

Wells and septic

Outside the towns and service districts, most homes are on private utilities. Order water and septic inspections early.

Commuter budgets

Many buyers work in Northern Virginia. Base your pre-approval on the payment you want to live with, including the commute, not the maximum you qualify for.

Before you make an offer in Fauquier County

  1. Get fully pre-approved. Well-priced homes in Warrenton and the commuter neighborhoods can draw more than one offer.
  2. Compare Loan Estimates on the same day. Rates move daily. Line up rate, points and lender fees.
  3. Describe the property accurately. Acreage, barns and accessory dwellings change how the loan is underwritten.
  4. Ask about VA first if you served. It usually beats every other low-down-payment option.

Warrenton and Fauquier County mortgage questions

What are the 2026 loan limits in Fauquier County, VA?

For a single-family home, both the conforming limit and the FHA limit are $1,249,125 in 2026, because Fauquier County is part of the Washington metro area. Limits are reset each year.

Can I get a mortgage on a horse property in Fauquier County?

Yes, when the property is primarily a residence. Barns, run-in sheds and pasture are fine. A commercial boarding or breeding operation is treated differently, so describe the property and any income from it at the start.

Is Warrenton, VA eligible for USDA loans?

USDA eligibility is set by address and changes across Fauquier County. Scott's team can check a specific property against the USDA map and the household income limits before you make an offer.

Can I use a VA loan to buy in Fauquier County?

Yes. Eligible veterans and service members can buy with no down payment, and with full entitlement there is no VA loan limit.
