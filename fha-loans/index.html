What an FHA loan gives you

The Federal Housing Administration insures the loan, which lets lenders approve buyers a conventional loan would turn away or price much higher.

3.5% down

Low down payment

3.5% of the price with a credit score of 580 or higher. Buyers with scores from 500 to 579 can qualify with 10% down.

Gift funds

The down payment can be a gift

A family member can give you the entire down payment. It does not have to come from your own savings.

Flexible credit

Forgiving on credit history

Lower scores, thin credit files and past problems such as collections or a bankruptcy are judged more leniently than on a conventional loan.

Seller credit

Sellers can help with closing costs

The seller can contribute up to 6% of the price toward your closing costs and prepaid items.

$1,249,125

High loan limit in Culpeper County

Streamline

Easy refinance later

If rates drop, the FHA streamline refinance lowers your rate with limited paperwork and usually no appraisal.

What FHA mortgage insurance costs

Mortgage insurance is the price of FHA's flexibility. It has two parts, and it is the main thing to weigh against a conventional loan.

Upfront: 1.75%

A one-time premium of 1.75% of the loan amount. Almost everyone adds it to the loan balance instead of paying it at closing.

Annual: 0.55%

For most 30-year loans with 3.5% down, 0.55% of the balance per year, split into your monthly payments.

How long it lasts

For the life of the loan with less than 10% down. For 11 years with 10% or more down.

The way out

Once you have about 20% equity and stronger credit, refinancing into a conventional loan removes it.

FHA or something else?

FHA is not automatically the cheapest low-down-payment loan. Check these before you choose.

  1. Veteran or service member? A VA loan has no down payment and no monthly mortgage insurance. It almost always beats FHA.
  2. Buying outside town limits? A USDA loan has no down payment and a lower annual fee, if the address and your household income qualify.
  3. Credit score in the 700s? A conventional loan with 3% to 5% down often has cheaper mortgage insurance, and it comes off on its own.
  4. Lower score, higher debts or a recent credit event? This is where FHA usually wins on both approval and payment.

FHA loans on Culpeper-area properties

The FHA appraiser checks that the home is safe, sound and secure. Most homes pass. These are the items that come up most on rural properties.

Wells and septic

Both must work properly. A water test may be required, and the well and septic need adequate separation.

Condition items

Peeling paint on homes built before 1978, missing handrails, roof leaks and broken windows have to be fixed before closing.

Manufactured homes

Eligible when built after June 1976, on a permanent foundation and titled as real estate.

Primary residence

FHA is for the home you live in. Two- to four-unit homes qualify if you live in one unit.

FHA loan questions from Culpeper buyers

What credit score do I need for an FHA loan in Virginia?

FHA allows a 3.5% down payment with a credit score of 580 or higher, and 10% down with a score from 500 to 579. Individual lenders can set higher minimums, so ask what applies before you apply.

How much is the down payment on an FHA loan?

The minimum is 3.5% of the purchase price for most buyers. The whole amount can come from a gift from a family member, and Virginia Housing assistance can cover part or all of it for buyers who qualify.

What is the FHA loan limit in Culpeper County?

For 2026 the FHA limit for a single-family home in Culpeper County is $1,249,125, because the county is part of the Washington metro area. HUD resets limits each calendar year.

How much is FHA mortgage insurance?

FHA charges a one-time upfront premium of 1.75% of the loan, which is usually added to the loan balance, plus an annual premium paid monthly. For most 30-year loans with 3.5% down the annual premium is 0.55% of the balance.

Does FHA mortgage insurance ever go away?

With less than 10% down, the annual premium stays for the life of the loan. With 10% or more down, it ends after 11 years. Many owners remove it sooner by refinancing into a conventional loan once they have enough equity.

Can I get an FHA loan on a home with a well and septic system?

Yes. The appraiser checks that the water supply and septic system work and that the home is safe and sound. A water test may be required, so schedule it early in the contract period.
