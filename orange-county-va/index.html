What makes an Orange County mortgage different

A few local details come up again and again on Orange County files.

HOA communities

Lake of the Woods and other planned communities charge association dues. Lenders count those dues in your monthly payment when they qualify you.

New construction

Builders are active on the east end. A builder's preferred lender may offer incentives, so compare the full Loan Estimate, not only the incentive.

Wells, septic and acreage

West of the Route 3 corridor most homes are on private utilities. Water tests and septic inspections should be ordered early.

Second homes near the lake

A second home has different down payment and occupancy rules than a primary residence. Say up front how you plan to use the property.

Before you make an offer in Orange County

  1. Know the dues. Get the HOA fee and any special assessments before you settle on a price range.
  2. Compare the builder's lender. Ask for a Loan Estimate from both on the same day and line up rate, points and fees.
  3. Check the address for USDA. Eligibility changes from one part of the county to another.
  4. Get fully pre-approved. Income and assets reviewed up front makes your offer stronger and your closing faster.

Orange County mortgage questions

Is Orange County, VA eligible for USDA loans?

Many addresses in Orange County are in USDA's eligible area, but it depends on the specific property and your household income. Scott's team can check an address in a few minutes.

Do HOA dues at Lake of the Woods affect how much I can borrow?

Yes. Lenders add association dues to your housing payment when calculating your debt-to-income ratio, so higher dues reduce the loan amount you qualify for.

Can I use a VA loan in Orange County?

Yes. Eligible veterans and service members can buy anywhere in the county with no down payment, including homes on wells and septic systems and newly built homes.

Should I use the builder's lender on a new home in Locust Grove?

Not automatically. Builder incentives can be worth taking, but compare a same-day Loan Estimate from an outside lender so you can see whether the rate and fees give the incentive back.

Can I finance a second home near Lake Anna?

Yes, usually with a conventional loan. Second homes need a larger down payment than a primary residence, and the home must be for your own use rather than a full-time rental.
