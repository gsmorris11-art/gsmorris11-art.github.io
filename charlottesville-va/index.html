What makes a Charlottesville mortgage different

A few things come up here that buyers in the surrounding counties rarely face.

Starting a new job

Buyers relocating for a position at UVA or the health system can often be approved on a signed offer letter before their first day. Ask how your loan type handles it.

City versus county

A few miles can change the FHA limit, USDA eligibility and the tax bill. Compare the full monthly payment, not just the price.

Condos

Condominiums need a project review by the lender, and FHA and VA keep their own approval lists. Check the building early.

Investment property

Buying a rental, including one a student will live in, follows investment-property rules with a larger down payment than a primary home.

Before you make an offer in Charlottesville

  1. Get fully pre-approved. Well-priced homes in the city and Crozet can draw multiple offers.
  2. Run the numbers in two locations. The same budget can buy differently in Albemarle than in Greene or Fluvanna, with different loan options.
  3. Check the address for USDA. Outside the urban ring it can remove the down payment.
  4. Compare lenders on the same day. Line up rate, points and lender fees on each Loan Estimate.

Charlottesville mortgage questions

What are the 2026 loan limits in Charlottesville, VA?

For a single-family home in Charlottesville and Albemarle County, the 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750 and the FHA limit is $598,000. HUD and FHFA reset limits each calendar year.

Is the Charlottesville area eligible for USDA loans?

The city and its immediate surroundings generally are not. Many addresses in Greene, Fluvanna, Nelson and Louisa counties are eligible, subject to USDA's household income limits. Eligibility is confirmed by address.

Can I buy a home before I start my new job at UVA?

Often, yes. Many loan programs allow approval based on a signed offer letter with a start date near closing. Your loan officer will confirm what your specific loan requires.

How much do I need to put down on a home in Charlottesville?

Eligible veterans can put nothing down with a VA loan. FHA starts at 3.5% down and some conventional loans at 3% for qualified first-time buyers. Investment properties need more.
